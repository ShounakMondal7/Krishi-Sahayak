GOVERNMENT OF INDIA — FARMER WELFARE SCHEMES AND SUBSIDIES 2024-25
Source: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
Document Reference: MoAFW/Schemes/2024-25/IN-01

This bulletin summarises all major central government schemes providing financial support, insurance,
credit, and technology to Indian farmers. Knowing these schemes is the farmer's right.

=======================================================================
SECTION 1: INCOME SUPPORT AND DIRECT BENEFIT
=======================================================================

1.1 PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
AIM: Direct income support to land-owning farmers.

BENEFIT: Rs 6,000 per year (3 equal instalments of Rs 2,000 every 4 months)
ELIGIBILITY:
- All land-owning farmer families (including small and marginal)
- Updated land records required (Bhunaksha/Bhulekh)
- Aadhaar-linked bank account required (mandatory)

EXCLUSIONS:
- Institutional land holders
- Farmer families holding constitutional positions (MP, MLA, etc.)
- Serving/retired government employees

HOW TO APPLY:
- Online: pmkisan.gov.in
- Common Service Centre (CSC): Bring Aadhaar + bank passbook + land records
- Status check: pmkisan.gov.in → "Beneficiary Status"

STATE ADDITIONS: Many states add additional amounts (Tamil Nadu +Rs 2,000, Telangana +Rs 10,000 Rythu Bandhu)

1.2 RYTHU BANDHU (TELANGANA):
- Rs 10,000/acre/season (Kharif + Rabi = Rs 20,000/year per acre)
- For all farmers in Telangana state
- Direct bank transfer before each season

1.3 KALIA SCHEME (ODISHA):
- Rs 10,000/year for cultivator farmers
- Rs 12,500 for landless agricultural labourers
- Additional: Free crop insurance

SECTION 2: CROP INSURANCE
=======================================================================

2.1 PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY)
India's main crop insurance scheme.

COVERAGE:
- Crop loss due to: Natural calamities (flood, drought, hail, cyclone)
- Prevented sowing due to adverse weather
- Post-harvest losses (for 14 days after harvest)
- Localized calamities (landslide, flood, hailstorm for specific field)

PREMIUM (Farmer's share):
- Kharif crops: 2% of Sum Insured (maximum)
- Rabi crops: 1.5% of Sum Insured
- Commercial/Horticultural crops: 5% of Sum Insured
- Government pays the rest

EXAMPLE: Rice insured at Rs 50,000/ha
- Farmer pays: 2% = Rs 1,000 (for Kharif)
- Government pays remaining premium (Rs 5,000–8,000)
- Farmer gets up to Rs 50,000 per ha if total crop loss

HOW TO ENROLL:
- Through CSC, bank branch, or insurance company
- Deadline: 2 weeks before sowing (Kharif) or end of November (Rabi)
- For loanee farmers: Auto-enrolled; can opt out if desired

CLAIM PROCESS:
- Crop loss >25%: Automatic assessment by government
- Specific field loss (hail/flood): Farmer must notify within 72 hours
  Call: Insurance company toll-free number (on policy document)
  Photo/video of damage very helpful

2.2 WEATHER BASED CROP INSURANCE:
- Payment triggered by weather station data (not crop inspection)
- Faster claim settlement (7–14 days vs months)
- For: Fruit crops, vegetables in many states

SECTION 3: CREDIT AND LOANS
=======================================================================

3.1 KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC)
BENEFIT: Short-term crop credit at 4% effective interest rate
(Normal bank rate: 7% | Government gives 3% interest subvention)

CREDIT LIMIT:
- Based on crop area × district-level scale of finance
- Typically Rs 50,000–5,00,000 for 5 acres

FEATURES:
- Revolving credit — withdraw as needed, repay after harvest
- No collateral needed up to Rs 1.6 lakh (for crop loans)
- Free personal accident insurance included (Rs 50,000)
- Free crop insurance through KCC link

ELIGIBILITY: Any farmer with land records or tenant farming proof
WHERE TO APPLY: Nearest branch of any nationalized bank, cooperative bank, or PACS

3.2 PRADHAN MANTRI MUDRA YOJANA (PMMY):
- For agriculture-allied activities (animal husbandry, fisheries, poultry)
- Loans up to Rs 10 lakh | No collateral up to Rs 10 lakh
- Shishu: up to Rs 50,000 | Kishor: Rs 50,000–5 lakh | Tarun: Rs 5–10 lakh

3.3 AGRI INFRASTRUCTURE FUND (AIF):
- Loans up to Rs 2 crore for post-harvest infrastructure
- 3% interest subvention
- For: Cold storage, warehouses, sorting units, primary processing

SECTION 4: IRRIGATION SUPPORT
=======================================================================

4.1 PM KRISHI SINCHAYEE YOJANA (PMKSY):
"Har Khet Ko Pani, More Crop Per Drop"

DRIP/SPRINKLER SUBSIDY (PDMC component):
| Farmer Category | Subsidy %    |
|----------------|-------------|
| Small/Marginal | 55%         |
| Other farmers  | 45%         |
Maximum subsidy: Rs 2 lakh per ha (drip), Rs 1 lakh per ha (sprinkler)

APPLICATION:
- State-level portal (different in each state)
- Documents: Land records, Aadhaar, bank account
- Quotation from empanelled manufacturer required

MICRO-IRRIGATION FUND (MIF):
- State governments can access Rs 5,000 crore fund from NABARD
- For farmer-level micro-irrigation subsidies

SECTION 5: INPUT SUBSIDIES
=======================================================================

5.1 SOIL HEALTH CARD (SHC):
- FREE soil testing every 2 years for all farmers
- Card shows N, P, K, pH, organic carbon, 8 micronutrients
- Specific fertilizer recommendation included
- Apply at nearest Soil Testing Laboratory (Krishi Vigyan Kendra or District Agriculture Office)

5.2 NEEM-COATED UREA:
- All urea in India is now mandatorily neem-coated (since 2015)
- Benefits: 10–15% more nitrogen efficiency | Reduces diversion to industry
- Price: Same as regular urea (Rs 266.50/50 kg bag, heavily subsidised)
- MRP fixed by government — cannot be sold above Rs 266.50

5.3 PM-PRANAM (Programme for Restoration, Awareness, Nourishment):
- Incentivizes states to reduce chemical fertilizer consumption
- States that reduce fertilizer subsidy draw can use savings for farmer schemes

5.4 NATIONAL FOOD SECURITY MISSION (NFSM):
- Free/subsidised certified seeds for rice, wheat, pulses, oilseeds
- Free bio-fertilizers, micronutrients under NFSM
- Apply: Nearest Block Agriculture Office (before Kharif/Rabi season)

SECTION 6: MARKETING AND PRICE SUPPORT
=======================================================================

6.1 MINIMUM SUPPORT PRICE (MSP) — 2024-25:
| Crop            | MSP (Rs/quintal) |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Paddy (Common)  | 2,300           |
| Wheat           | 2,275           |
| Maize           | 2,090           |
| Soybean         | 4,892           |
| Groundnut       | 6,783           |
| Cotton (Medium) | 7,121           |
| Cotton (Long)   | 7,521           |
| Tur (Pigeon Pea)| 7,000           |
| Moong           | 8,682           |
| Urad            | 7,400           |
| Lentil (Masoor) | 6,425           |
| Mustard         | 5,650           |
| Sugarcane (FRP) | 340 (per qtl)   |

HOW TO SELL AT MSP:
- Register on eNAM (enam.gov.in) — online mandi platform
- Bring produce to nearest APMC/Government Procurement Centre
- Required documents: Aadhaar card, bank account, crop registration
- PM Fasal Bima Yojana — always enroll to protect MSP recovery

6.2 eNAM (Electronic National Agriculture Market):
- Online trading platform linking 1,361 mandis across India
- Farmer can sell from home state mandi to buyers across India
- Better price discovery | Transparent bidding
- Registration: enam.gov.in or at nearest mandi office

6.3 PM-AASHA (Farmer income protection):
- Price Deficiency Payment Scheme: Farmer gets difference between MSP and mandi price
- State procurement when mandi price < MSP for notified crops

SECTION 7: TECHNOLOGY AND EDUCATION SUPPORT
=======================================================================

7.1 KRISHI VIGYAN KENDRA (KVK):
- 731 KVKs across India (1 per district target)
- Services:
  * Free soil testing
  * Training on new varieties and techniques
  * Demonstration plots
  * Weather-based agro-advisory (weekly)
  * Subsidised/free seed mini-kits of new varieties
  * Rented farm machinery (tractors, seed drills, threshers)
  * Goat, poultry, fishery guidance

- IFFCO Kisan: Weather + market info app
