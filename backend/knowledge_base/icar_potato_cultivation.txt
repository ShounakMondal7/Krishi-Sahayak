1. MAJOR VARIETIES & CULTIVATION CHARACTERISTICS
Potato (Solanum tuberosum) is one of the most important cash and food crops in India, widely cultivated in Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Punjab, and Gujarat.

Key High-Yielding ICAR-CPRI Varieties:
- Kufri Pukhraj: Early maturing (70-90 days), high-yielding, yellow flesh, wide adaptability across North and Central Indian plains. Tolerant to early blight.
- Kufri Jyoti: Medium maturing (90-100 days), oval white tubers, highly popular for table consumption and hill cultivation. Moderately resistant to late blight.
- Kufri Bahar: Medium maturing (90-100 days), large round-oval tubers, white flesh, excellent for main season cultivation in Uttar Pradesh and Haryana plains.
- Kufri Chipsona (1 & 3): Dedicated processing varieties with high dry matter (>21%) and low reducing sugars, highly valued for chips and processing industries.
- Kufri Mohan: High yielding, medium maturing variety with attractive white-cream tubers, capable of producing high proportion of marketable Grade-A tubers.

2. MAJOR DISEASES & SCIENTIFIC MANAGEMENT
Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans):
- Identification & Symptoms: Water-soaked irregular lesions on leaf margins and tips during cold, cloudy, humid weather (>80% RH, temp 10-20°C). White cottony fungal growth develops on the undersides of leaves early morning. Lesions turn purplish-brown to black, rotting entire foliage within days.
- Sanitation & Organic: Use certified disease-free seed tubers. Avoid overhead irrigation and waterlogging. Spray Trichoderma viride or 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) preventively.
- Chemical Treatment: Preventive spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0-2.5 g/liter of water (30-40 g per 15-liter knapsack pump). Upon first appearance of late blight, curative spray of Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/liter (40 g per 15L pump) or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold) @ 2.0-2.5 g/liter (30-40 g per 15L pump). Follow with Fenamidone 10% + Mancozeb 50% WG @ 2.5 g/liter.
- Precaution: 10-14 days pre-harvest interval (PHI). Spray early morning or late afternoon in calm conditions.

Early Blight (Alternaria solani):
- Identification & Symptoms: Brown-to-black necrotic spots with characteristic concentric rings ("target board" pattern) on older leaves.
- Management: Spray Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/liter (30 g per 15L pump) or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/liter (15 ml per 15L pump).

Aphids (Myzus persicae) & Potato Tuber Moth (Phthorimaea operculella):
- Identification: Sucking sap, transmitting potato leaf roll virus (PLRV); tuber moth larvae bore into tubers in field and country storage.
- Management: Yellow sticky traps (15-20 traps/ha). Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 ml/liter (5 ml per 15-liter pump) or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.4 g/liter (6 g per 15-liter pump).

3. HARVESTING, CURING & COLD STORAGE MANAGEMENT
- Haulm Cutting: Cut haulms (vines) 10-15 days before harvest when crops mature to harden tuber skins and reduce bruising.
- Curing: Cure harvested tubers in a cool, well-aerated shaded shed at 15-18°C with 85-90% relative humidity for 10-15 days to allow subberization and healing of skin wounds.
- Cold Storage Parameters: Commercial table potatoes must be stored in cold storage units maintained strictly at 3-4°C with 90-95% relative humidity to prevent sprouting and shrinkage. Processing potatoes are stored at 8-10°C with sprout inhibitors (CIPC).
- Bagging: Pack in clean 50 kg jute or ventilated Leno mesh bags; avoid synthetic non-aerated plastic sacks.
