ICAR COMPREHENSIVE PROTECTED CULTIVATION, POLYHOUSE & MULCHING MANUAL
================================================================================
Grounded in ICAR-CPCT (Centre for Protected Cultivation Technology, New Delhi) & NHB Guidelines.

SECTION 1: PROTECTED STRUCTURES & DESIGN ENGINEERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Protected cultivation modifies microclimate parameters (ambient temperature, solar radiation, humidity, wind velocity, and pest vectors), enabling 3x to 5x higher marketable yields of high-value crops, off-season premium production, and 90% water saving under precision drip fertigation.
1. Naturally Ventilated Polyhouse (NVPH - Sawtooth / Multispan Gable Type):
   - Cladding Material: 200-micron UV-stabilized polyethylene film with anti-drip, anti-fog, and thermal diffuse properties (diffuses 50-60% light to prevent scorch).
   - Side & Top Vents: 40-mesh nylon insect-proof net installed along continuous 3-meter side curtains and 1-meter roof ridge vents to keep out aphids, thrips, and whiteflies while allowing natural thermal convective airflow.
   - Foundation: GI galvanized tubular pipes (Class B, minimum 2 mm thickness) anchored in concrete footings to resist wind gusts up to 120 km/h.
2. Shade-Net House (Flat Roof / Dome Type):
   - Netting: 50% Green/Black UV-stabilized agro-shade net for semi-tropical zones; 35% for temperate zones; 75% for foliage ornamentals and nursery propagation.
   - Cost: Lower initial capital outlay (₹350 to ₹450 per sq. meter vs ₹850 to ₹1,000 per sq. meter for NVPH).
3. Walk-in Tunnels & Low Tunnels:
   - Polyethylene film (25-50 micron) supported on GI wire arches (height 45-60 cm) over vegetable nursery beds and off-season winter cucurbits (watermelon, muskmelon) to protect against frost and advance harvest by 30-40 days.

SECTION 2: AGRICULTURAL PLASTIC MULCHING TECHNOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Plastic mulching conserves 35-45% soil moisture, prevents soil crusting, suppresses 95% weed growth, prevents splash dispersal of soil-borne blight pathogens onto leaves, and maintains optimal root-zone soil temperature.
- Film Thickness Specifications:
  * Short-duration Vegetables (Tomato, Chilli, Capsicum, Cucumber, Melons): 25 to 30 micron.
  * Medium-duration Crops (Strawberry, Papaya, Banana): 50 micron.
  * Orchard Tree Basins (Pomegranate, Citrus, Guava): 100 micron.
- Color Dynamics:
  * Silver-on-Black (Industry Gold Standard): Top silver layer reflects 30% solar radiation (cooling soil in summer) and reflects UV light into lower canopy, confusing aphids and whiteflies. Bottom black layer completely blocks sunlight, killing germinating weed seeds.
  * Black-on-Black: Used in cold winter climates to trap thermal energy and elevate root-zone temperature.
  * Transparent / Clear: Used exclusively for summer soil solarization (4-6 weeks under May-June sun) to heat top 5 cm soil to 50-55°C, sterilizing nematodes, damping-off fungi, and weed seeds.
- Bed Preparation & Laying:
  * Form raised beds of 90-100 cm top width, 15-20 cm height, and 40-50 cm walking pathways.
  * Lay inline drip irrigation lateral (16 mm diameter with 2 to 4 LPH drippers spaced at 30-40 cm) along the center of the bed before laying mulch.
  * Stretch mulch film tightly across the bed, burying both edges 10-15 cm deep in soil along the furrows to anchor against wind lift.
  * Punch circular 5-7 cm transplanting holes at desired plant spacing using heated pipe cutters.

SECTION 3: HIGH-VALUE GREENHOUSE CROPS & DRIP FERTIGATION SCHEDULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Color Capsicum (Yellow & Red Sweet Bell Pepper - Bachata, Inspiration):
   - Spacing: Paired row system on 90 cm bed (45 cm between rows, 30 cm between plants; approx. 14,000 plants per acre / 3,500 plants per 1000 sq. meter).
   - Training & Pruning: 2-leader or 4-leader vertical trellis training using nylon twine suspended from overhead GI support cables; prune all suckers and tertiary branches.
2. Parthenocarpic English Slicing Cucumber (Gypsy, Multistar):
   - Flowers require zero bee pollination (100% female flowers). Yields 25 to 35 tonnes per 1000 sq. meter over a 120-day cropping cycle.
3. Dutch Cut Roses (Top Secret, Passion, Avalanche):
   - Planting density: 7 to 8 plants per sq. meter on raised gravel/soil beds; requires bent-shoot (bending) canopy architecture to build strong root starch reserves for high-stem commercial flushes.

Precision Fertigation Regimen (per 1000 sq. meter greenhouse per day):
- Vegetative Phase (Day 15 to 45): Inject 19:19:19 (Polyfeed) @ 1.5 kg + Magnesium Sulfate @ 500g in Drip Tank A; Micronutrient mixture (Chelated Zn, Fe, B, Mn, Cu) @ 50g in Tank B. Alternate days.
- Flowering & Fruit Setting (Day 46 to 90): Inject 12:61:00 (MAP) @ 1.0 kg + 00:52:34 (MKP) @ 1.5 kg + Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) @ 1.5 kg.
- Heavy Harvesting Phase (Day 91 onwards): Inject Calcium Nitrate @ 1.5 kg (Tank A) and Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) @ 2.0 kg + 00:00:50 @ 1.0 kg (Tank B). Keep electrical conductivity (EC) of drip emitter discharge between 1.5 and 2.0 mS/cm and pH between 5.8 and 6.5.

SECTION 4: GREENHOUSE INTEGRATED PEST & DISEASE MANAGEMENT (IPM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
High relative humidity (80-90%) inside polyhouses creates favorable microclimates for powdery mildew, downy mildew, and sap-sucking pests:
- Yellow Sticky Traps: Install 20 bright yellow sticky sheets per 1000 sq. meter at canopy height to capture adult whiteflies (Bemisia tabaci) and leafminers.
- Blue Sticky Traps: Install 15 blue sticky sheets per 1000 sq. meter for early detection and trapping of Western Flower Thrips (Frankliniella occidentalis).
- Red Spider Mite (Tetranychus urticae - web-spinning on leaf undersides):
  * Spray Spiromesifen 22.9% SC (Oberon) @ 15 ml per 15-liter pump OR Fenazaquin 10% EC @ 30 ml per 15L pump.
- Whitefly & Thrips Control:
  * Spray Cyantraniliprole 10.26% OD (Benevia) @ 25-30 ml per 15-liter pump OR Diafenthiuron 50% WP (Pegasus) @ 18-20g per 15L pump.
- Powdery Mildew Control:
  * Spray Hexaconazole 5% SC @ 30 ml per 15-liter pump OR Difenoconazole 25% EC (Score) @ 10 ml per 15L pump.
- National Horticulture Board (NHB) & MIDH Subsidies:
  * 50% capital subsidy (up to ₹44.75 Lakh per acre / ₹1120 per sq. meter) provided under MIDH / NHB Scheme for establishing polyhouses, shade nets, and packhouses with cold chain support. Apply on nhb.gov.in.
