India is the 2nd largest fish producing country in the world (17.5+ million metric tonnes), with Andhra Pradesh,
West Bengal, Gujarat, Kerala, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, and Assam as top producers. Aquaculture is the fastest growing
food sector, generating over ₹60,000 crore in annual seafood exports (dominated by frozen shrimp).

SECTION 1: FRESHWATER CARP POLYCULTURE (Composite Fish Culture)
=======================================================================

Composite fish culture utilizes different ecological water layers to maximize pond productivity without food competition.

1.1 RECOMMENDED SPECIES COMBINATION (Six-Species Culture):
- Surface Feeders (utilize phytoplankton & zooplankton at water surface):
  * Catla (Catla catla): 20% stocking ratio; fastest growing Indian major carp.
  * Silver Carp (Hypophthalmichthys molitrix): 10–15% stocking ratio; controls algal blooms.
- Column Feeders (utilize organic detritus & plankton in mid-water column):
  * Rohu (Labeo rohita): 30–35% stocking ratio; most preferred food fish in India; Jayanti Rohu (CIFA improved breed
    grows 17% faster per generation with high resistance to aeromoniasis).
  * Grass Carp (Ctenopharyngodon idella): 10% stocking ratio; feeds on submerged aquatic weeds (Hydrilla, Najas).
- Bottom Feeders (consume benthic organisms, decaying matter at pond floor):
  * Mrigal (Cirrhinus mrigala): 15–20% stocking ratio.
  * Common Carp (Cyprinus carpio): 10–15% stocking ratio; scavenger, stirs bottom nutrients.

1.2 POND PREPARATION & LIMING:
- Pond Drying & Desilting: Dry pond bed until cracked to eliminate pathogens and predators.
- Liming (Mandatory):
  * Agricultural Lime (CaCO₃) @ 250–350 kg/ha for neutral soils; 500–1000 kg/ha for acidic soils (pH < 6.5).
  * Benefits: Neutralizes acidity, stabilizes pH, kills parasites, clarifies water turbidity.
- Manuring & Fertilization:
  * Raw Cattle Dung @ 5–10 tonnes/ha incorporated 15 days before stocking.
  * Inorganic: Urea @ 25 kg/ha + Single Super Phosphate (SSP) @ 50 kg/ha monthly.

1.3 STOCKING DENSITY & FEED MANAGEMENT:
- Fingerling Size: Stock healthy fingerlings of 8–10 cm length (minimum 25–30 grams).
- Density: 7,500 to 10,000 fingerlings per hectare (semi-intensive pond system).
- Feeding Protocol:
  * Supplementary Feed: Rice bran and Mustard oil cake (1:1 ratio) @ 2–3% of total fish biomass daily.
  * Extruded Floating Pellets (28% crude protein): Reduces water pollution, improves Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR 1.2–1.5).

SECTION 2: CRITICAL WATER QUALITY PARAMETERS (The Pond Health Index)
=======================================================================

Farmers must test these parameters weekly to prevent mass fish mortality:

| Parameter              | Optimal Range        | Hazard Level        | Immediate Corrective Action              |
|------------------------|----------------------|---------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO)  | 5.0 – 8.0 mg/L       | < 3.0 mg/L (Gasping)| Turn on aerators / paddlewheels immediately; pump fresh water |
| Water pH               | 7.5 – 8.5            | < 6.5 or > 9.0      | Apply agricultural lime (if low) or alum/gypsum (if high) |
| Water Temperature      | 25°C – 32°C          | < 18°C or > 35°C    | Increase water depth to 1.8–2.0 meters   |
| Transparency (Secchi)  | 30 – 40 cm           | < 20 cm (Bloom)     | Stop fertilization immediately; exchange 25% water |
| Total Alkalinity       | 100 – 200 mg/L       | < 50 mg/L           | Add quicklime or sodium bicarbonate      |
| Ammonia (Un-ionized)   | < 0.05 mg/L          | > 0.1 mg/L          | Add Zeolite @ 50 kg/ha; reduce feeding   |

SECTION 3: MAJOR FISH DISEASES & CONTROL
=======================================================================

3.1 EPIZOOTIC ULCERATIVE SYNDROME (EUS / Red Spot Disease):
- Causative agent: Oomycete fungus Aphanomyces invadans + secondary Aeromonas hydrophila.
- Prevalent in winter (December–February) following sudden water temperature drop.
- Symptoms: Red circular erosions on skin that develop into deep, necrotic, bleeding ulcers.
- CIFA Approved Treatment:
  * Apply CIFAX formulation @ 1.0 Litre per hectare-meter water depth.
  * Water sanitation: Apply Potassium Permanganate (KMnO₄) @ 2–3 mg/L or Quicklime @ 200 kg/ha.

3.2 ARGULOSIS (Fish Louse / Argulus foliaceus):
- Disc-shaped crustacean parasite visible crawling on skin, gills, and fins; causes irritation, rubbing against dykes.
- Management:
  * Apply Ivermectin 1% @ 1.0 ml per kg feed for 3 consecutive days.
  * Or spray Emamectin benzoate (0.2% EC) @ 100 ml per hectare-meter.
  * Install bamboo poles in pond: Argulus lays eggs on hard surfaces; remove poles weekly and scrape eggs off.

3.3 GILL ROT (Branchiomyces):
- Gills become pale, marbled, necrotic, and disintegrate.
- Management: Add Copper Sulphate @ 0.5 mg/L; aerate continuously.

SECTION 4: BIOFLOC & RECIRCULATING AQUACULTURE (RAS)
=======================================================================

Modern zero-water-exchange intensive technologies for land-constrained farmers.

4.1 BIOFLOC TECHNOLOGY (BFT):
- Principle: Carbon-Nitrogen ratio (C:N ratio) maintained at 12:1 to 15:1 by adding carbon source (molasses / jaggery).
- Heterotrophic bacteria convert toxic ammonia into edible microbial protein ("Biofloc").
- Stocking: High density (Pangasius: 50–70 fish/m³; Tilapia: 80–100 fish/m³; Vannamei shrimp: 150–200/m³).
- Floc Volume: Maintained at 15–25 ml/L (measured using Imhoff Cone).
- Electricity: Continuous aeration (blower / venturi) non-negotiable 24×7.

SECTION 5: COASTAL BRACKISHWATER SHRIMP FARMING (Litopenaeus vannamei)
=======================================================================

Andhra Pradesh (Nellore, Krishna, Godavari) & Gujarat lead India's shrimp revolution.

5.1 BIOSECURITY & SPF SEED:
- Use only Specific Pathogen Free (SPF) Post-Larvae (PL-10 to PL-12) from CAA-registered hatcheries.
- Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) screening for WSSV, EHP, and IHHNV mandatory before stocking.
- Install crab fencing and bird scaring netting around ponds to block viral vectors.

5.2 WHITE SPOT SYNDROME VIRUS (WSSV):
- Devastating viral disease; causes 100% mortality in 3–5 days.
- Symptoms: White spots (0.5–2 mm) inside carapace; reddish body discolouration; lethargy at pond edges.
- Management: NO CHEMICAL CURE. Emergency harvest if commercial size reached. Prevent vector entry.

SECTION 6: PRADHAN MANTRI MATSYA SAMPADA YOJANA (PMMSY)
=======================================================================
- Flagship ₹20,050 crore government scheme for fisheries:
  * Subsidy: 40% for General Category; 60% for SC/ST and Women beneficiaries.
  * Eligible Projects: Construction of new ponds, Biofloc units, RAS, fish feed mills, solar aerators,
    refrigerated transport vehicles, ornamental fish units.
