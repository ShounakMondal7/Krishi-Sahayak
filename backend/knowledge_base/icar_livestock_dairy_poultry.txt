ICAR TECHNICAL BULLETIN — Dairy, Livestock & Poultry Husbandry Advisory for Indian Farmers
Source: ICAR-National Dairy Research Institute (NDRI), Karnal & ICAR-Central Avian Research Institute (CARI), Izatnagar
Document Reference: ICAR-LIVESTOCK/Tech/2024/PAN-IN-01

Livestock contributes ~30% of total agricultural GDP in India and serves as the primary livelihood security net
for over 73 million rural households. India ranks #1 in global milk production (230+ million tonnes) and #3 in egg
production. This bulletin provides practical guidelines for dairy cattle, buffalo, goat rearing, and poultry.

=======================================================================
SECTION 1: DAIRY CATTLE & BUFFALO MANAGEMENT
=======================================================================

1.1 RECOMMENDED BREEDS:
A. Indigenous Dairy Cattle (Heat, tick & disease tolerant; A2 milk profile):
   - Gir (Gujarat/Maharashtra): Yield 3,000–4,500 L/lactation; distinct convex forehead, long pendulous ears.
   - Sahiwal (Punjab/Haryana/UP): Best dairy zebu breed; yield 3,200–4,000 L/lactation; reddish-dun color.
   - Red Sindhi & Tharparkar (Rajasthan): Exceptionally drought & heat hardy.
   - Crossbred Cattle (HF × Gir / Jersey × Sahiwal): High yield (4,500–6,500 L/lactation), but need cooling & shelter.
B. Dairy Buffaloes (Highest fat content 6.5–8.5%):
   - Murrah (Haryana/Punjab): "Black Gold", world's premier dairy buffalo; tightly curled horns; 2,500–3,600 L.
   - Jaffarabadi (Gujarat): Heavy draft & high-fat milk producer (7.5–9% fat).
   - Mehsana, Surti, Nili-Ravi.

1.2 SCIENTIFIC FEEDING & BALANCED RATION:
- Thumb Rule for Daily Feed Intake: Dry Matter (DM) requirement is 2.5–3.0 kg per 100 kg body weight.
- Daily Ration Composition for a 400 kg Cow yielding 10 L milk:
  * Green Fodder: 25–30 kg (Maize, Sorghum, Hybrid Napier / CO-4, Lucerne, Berseem).
  * Dry Roughage: 4–5 kg (Paddy straw or wheat straw; always treat with 4% urea to boost digestibility).
  * Concentrate Mixture: 1.5 kg (for body maintenance) + 400 g per litre of milk produced (e.g., 4.0 kg for 10 L).
  * Mineral Mixture (NDRI Type-II): 50–60 grams daily (MANDATORY to prevent silent heat, repeat breeding, and pica).
  * Clean Drinking Water: 60–80 litres daily (free access).

1.3 SILAGE MAKING (Green Fodder Security for Dry Seasons):
- Best crops: Green maize or fodder sorghum harvested at milk-to-dough stage (30–35% dry matter).
- Process:
  * Chop fodder to 2–3 cm length.
  * Pack tightly in concrete trench / silo pit or heavy 200-micron silo bags to expel all air (anaerobic).
  * Add 1% jaggery + 0.5% salt or commercial lactic acid bacterial culture.
  * Seal airtight with plastic sheet and soil weight.
  * Ferments in 45–60 days; remains nutritious for up to 18–24 months; pH should be 3.8–4.2 with sweet-sour aroma.

1.4 MASTITIS MANAGEMENT (The #1 Economic Loss in Dairy):
- Symptoms: Swollen, hot, painful quarter; watery, clotty, or discolored milk; reduced yield.
- Prevention (California Mastitis Test — CMT):
  * Test all quarters weekly using CMT paddle to detect subclinical mastitis before clots appear.
  * Post-milking Teat Dipping: Dip all 4 teats in 0.5% Povidone-Iodine solution immediately after every milking.
  * Dry Cow Therapy: Infuse long-acting intramammary antibiotic at start of dry period.
- Treatment: Intramammary antibiotic infusion + systemic anti-inflammatory (Flunixin / Meloxicam) under veterinary guidance.
  NEVER consume milk from cows undergoing antibiotic treatment during withholding period (3–5 days).

SECTION 2: NATIONAL LIVESTOCK VACCINATION & DEWORMING CALENDAR
=======================================================================

Failure to vaccinate can cause 100% herd wipeout. All farmers must adhere to this schedule:

| Disease                 | Target Animals         | Primary Age     | Booster / Regular Schedule |
|-------------------------|------------------------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| Foot & Mouth (FMD)      | Cattle, Buffalo, Goats | 4 months        | Every 6 months (Bi-annual) |
| Haemorrhagic Septicaemia| Cattle, Buffalo        | 6 months        | Annually (Pre-monsoon, May)|
| Black Quarter (BQ)      | Cattle, Buffalo        | 6 months        | Annually (Pre-monsoon, May)|
| Brucellosis (Bruvax)    | Female calves ONLY     | 4 to 8 months   | ONCE IN LIFETIME (Calfhood)|
| Peste des Petits (PPR)  | Goats & Sheep          | 3 months        | Once every 3 years         |
| Enterotoxaemia (ET)     | Goats & Sheep          | 4 months        | Annually (Pre-monsoon)     |
| Ranikhet (RD-F1 / LaSota)| Poultry (Chicks)      | Day 5–7         | Day 28 (Lasota / RD-VK)    |

DEWORMING PROTOCOL:
- Calves: Deworm on Day 7, 14, 21, 30 with Piperazine or Albendazole; then monthly up to 6 months.
- Adult Cattle & Buffalo: Deworm with broad-spectrum anthelmintic (Oxyclozanide + Levamisole or Fenbendazole)
  twice a year: Pre-monsoon (May) and Post-monsoon (October).
- Pregnant animals: Fenbendazole is safe in pregnancy; avoid Albendazole in first trimester.

SECTION 3: GOAT & SHEEP REARING (Small Ruminants)
=======================================================================

3.1 TOP MEAT & DUAL-PURPOSE GOAT BREEDS:
- Jamunapari & Beetal (UP/Punjab): Tall, long Roman nose, high twinning rate (60%), 2.0–2.5 L milk/day.
- Osmanabadi (Maharashtra/Karnataka): Highly disease resistant, black coat, adapts to harsh drylands.
- Sirohi (Rajasthan/Gujarat): Compact brown-spotted body, rapid weight gain in intensive stalls.
- Malabari / Tellicherry (Kerala): High prolificacy (triplets common), adapts to humid tropical rainfall.
- Black Bengal (West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, Odisha): World's best skin quality, multiple kidding (3–4 kids), succulent meat.

3.2 STALL-FED (INTENSIVE) GOAT FARMING:
- Elevated slatted floor housing (wood or plastic slats 1.5–2.0 feet above ground):
  * Keeps animals dry, dung falls through, eliminates 85% of coccidiosis and internal parasite infections.
- Feeding: Green fodder 4–5 kg + 200–300 g concentrate feed/day. Feed mineral mixture daily.
- Mortality reduction in kids: Ensure 100% colostrum feeding within 2 hours of birth (at 10% of birth weight).

SECTION 4: BACKYARD & COMMERCIAL POULTRY
=======================================================================

4.1 IMPROVED INDIGENOUS / DUAL-PURPOSE BREEDS:
- Kadaknath (MP/Chhattisgarh): GI Tagged; black meat, bones, and organs; low cholesterol (<0.73%), high protein (>25%).
  * High market premium: Live bird ₹600–₹1,000/kg; eggs ₹20–₹35/piece.
- Aseel (AP/Odisha/Tamil Nadu): Majestic game breed, high fighting spirit, sturdy, disease-hardy.
- CARI Nirbheek, CARI Shyama, Vanaraja, Gramapriya, Srinidhi:
  * Lay 160–200 eggs/year (vs 50–60 eggs of desi local birds).
  * Require minimal scavenging supplement; escape predators due to alert flight capability.

4.2 BIOSECURITY & DISEASE PREVENTION:
- Ranikhet Disease (Newcastle Disease): Watery greenish diarrhea, twisted neck, respiratory distress, 90% mortality.
  * Mandatory: Vaccinate chicks on Day 5 (F1 intraocular drops) and Day 28 (LaSota).
- Infectious Bursal Disease (Gumboro / IBD): Vaccinate on Day 14 (IBD intermediate strain drops).
- Coccidiosis: Bloody droppings; maintain bone-dry litter bedding; use Coccidiostat (Amprolium) in drinking water.

SECTION 5: GOVERNMENT SUBSIDIES & LIVESTOCK SCHEMES
=======================================================================
- National Livestock Mission (NLM): 50% capital subsidy (up to ₹50 lakh for sheep/goat breeding units;
  up to ₹25 lakh for poultry breeding farms). Apply online at: nlm.udyamimitra.in.
- Rashtriya Gokul Mission: Subsidized sex-sorted semen (ensures 90% female calves born).
- KCC for Animal Husbandry: Working capital loan up to ₹2 lakh at 4% interest rate.
