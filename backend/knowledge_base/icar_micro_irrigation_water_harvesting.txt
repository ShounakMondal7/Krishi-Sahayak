SECTION 1: DRIP IRRIGATION SYSTEM DESIGN & SPECIFICATIONS
=======================================================================
1. Component Layout & Filtration:
   - Head Control Unit: Pump unit, Screen filter (120 mesh / 130 micron for sand/organic particles), Disc filter (secondary safety), Venturi fertilizer injector or fertilizer tank, and Pressure Relief Valve (PRV).
   - Pressure Regulators: Operating pressure must be maintained at 1.0 to 1.5 kg/cm² (15 to 22 psi) at the field sub-main entrance.
   - Mainline & Sub-main: Rigid PVC pipes (63 mm or 75 mm outer diameter, 4 to 6 kg/cm² pressure rating) buried 1.5 to 2.0 feet underground to prevent UV and mechanical damage.
   - Laterals: UV-stabilized LLDPE tubes (12 mm or 16 mm outer diameter, Class-2).

2. Crop-Specific Emitter Spacing & Discharge Rates:
   - Vegetable Crops (Tomato, Chilli, Brinjal, Capsicum):
     * Lateral spacing: 1.2 m to 1.5 m (one lateral per bed).
     * Emitter spacing: 30 cm to 40 cm along lateral.
     * Emitter discharge rate: 2.0 liters per hour (LPH) inline pressure-compensating (PC) drippers.
   - Cotton, Maize & Sugarcane:
     * Sugarcane: Paired-row planting (30:120:30 cm) with one lateral serving two rows. Emitter spacing 50 cm, 4.0 LPH discharge.
     * Cotton: Lateral spacing 1.2 m, emitter spacing 40 cm, 2.0 to 2.4 LPH.
   - Horticultural Orchards (Mango, Guava, Citrus, Pomegranate):
     * Circular loop lateral around each tree canopy basin with 2 to 4 online button drippers per tree (4.0 to 8.0 LPH each).

SECTION 2: LATERAL MAINTENANCE, FLUSHING & ACID TREATMENT
=======================================================================
1. Routine Fortnightly Sub-Main & Lateral Flushing:
   - Open end-caps (flush valves) of all lateral lines while pump is operating.
   - Allow water to run freely for 1 to 2 minutes until completely clear water emerges without sand or silt deposits.

2. Acid Treatment for Mineral Clogging (Calcium/Magnesium Carbonates):
   - Frequency: Every 3 to 6 months depending on groundwater hardness (water pH > 7.5 or total dissolved solids > 1000 ppm).
   - Chemical Choice: Hydrochloric acid (Commercial HCl 30–33%) or Technical Nitric acid (HNO3 60%).
   - Procedure:
     * Inject acid via Venturi system until water coming out of the furthest lateral has a pH of 3.5 to 4.0 (tested with pH paper).
     * Shut off the irrigation system and let the acid solution stand inside the pipes and drippers for 24 hours.
     * Next morning, flush the system thoroughly with clean water for 30 minutes before irrigating crops.

3. Chlorine (Bleach) Treatment for Algal & Bacterial Slime:
   - Inject Sodium Hypochlorite (liquid bleach) at 20 to 50 ppm available chlorine.
   - Retain in system for 2 to 4 hours, then flush completely to prevent dripper bio-clogging.

SECTION 3: SPRINKLER IRRIGATION SYSTEMS
=======================================================================
1. Portable Micro-Sprinkler & Overhead Systems:
   - Suitable Crops: Wheat, Mustard, Groundnut, Gram, Soybeans, Pulses, and Fodder grasses.
   - Operating Pressure: 2.5 to 3.5 kg/cm².
   - Nozzle Spacing: 12 m × 12 m grid layout for standard rotary impact sprinklers (nozzle size 4.0–4.5 mm, discharge 1.2 to 1.8 m³/hour).
   - Water Saving: 35% to 45% water saving compared to surface flood irrigation; eliminates bund erosion and waterlogging.

SECTION 4: FARM PONDS (KHET TALAB) & RAINWATER HARVESTING
=======================================================================
1. Farm Pond Design Standards:
   - Size Recommendation for 5-Acre Holding:
     * Dimensions: Top 30 m × 30 m, Bottom 20 m × 20 m, Depth 3.0 meters (1.5:1 side slope).
     * Water Storage Capacity: 1,800 to 2,000 cubic meters (m³), sufficient for 2 protective supplemental irrigations for 5 acres of Kharif/Rabi crops.
   - Catchment & Silt Trap:
     * Construct an inlet masonry silt trap (Desilting chamber) at entry to deposit suspended clay before clean water enters main pond.
     * Emergency spillway designed 30 cm below embankment crest to safely discharge excess monsoon runoff.

2. Pond Seepage Prevention:
   - Polythene Lining: 500-micron multilayer cross-laminated UV-stabilized geomembrane (LDPE / HDPE conforming to IS:15351 standards).
   - Installation: Smooth pond bed, remove sharp stones, spread 5 cm fine sand cushion, anchor sheet in 50 cm × 50 cm trench on perimeter berm.
   - Natural Clay Lining: 15 cm thick puddle clay layer mixed with bentonite (10 kg/m²) where plastic film is unavailable.

3. Groundwater Recharge Wells:
   - Filter Pit Dimensions: 2.5 m diameter × 3 m depth around existing defunct open well or casing pipe.
   - Filter Media Layers: Bottom 50 cm coarse gravel (40-60 mm), middle 50 cm small gravel (20-40 mm), top 50 cm coarse sand.

SECTION 5: PMKSY (PER DROP MORE CROP) SUBSIDY GUIDELINES
=======================================================================
1. Subsidy Structure:
   - Small & Marginal Farmers (< 2 Hectares / 5 Acres landholding): Up to 55% financial assistance on indicative benchmark cost.
   - Other / Medium-Large Farmers (> 2 Hectares): Up to 45% financial assistance on benchmark cost.
   - Special Category States (Northeastern, Himalayan states, Union Territories): Up to 60-70% total subsidy support.

2. Benchmark Costs (Reference Indicative Matrix):
   - Drip Irrigation (Wide Spacing Orchards 5m×5m): ~₹25,000 to ₹35,000 per hectare.
   - Drip Irrigation (Close Spacing Vegetables 1.2m×0.6m): ~₹90,000 to ₹1,15,000 per hectare.
   - Portable Sprinkler System (63 mm pipes with accessories): ~₹20,000 to ₹25,000 per hectare.

3. Application Workflow:
   - Apply online via State Department of Horticulture / Agriculture DBT portal (e.g., e-Udyan UP, Mahadbt Maharashtra, HORTNET).
   - Mandatory Documents: Aadhaar card, 7/12 extract / Khasra-Khatauni, active bank passbook, soil & water test report, farm layout sketch.
   - Verification: Geotagged physical inspection before work order and post-installation GPS camera verification by Assistant Director of Horticulture.
