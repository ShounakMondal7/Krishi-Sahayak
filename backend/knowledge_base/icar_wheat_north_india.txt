ICAR TECHNICAL BULLETIN — Wheat Cultivation Guide for North Indian Farmers
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Wheat and Barley Research (IIWBR), Karnal, Haryana
Document Reference: ICAR-IIWBR/Tech/2024/PB-HY-UP-01

Wheat is the second most important food crop of India after rice. Punjab, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh
together contribute about 70% of total wheat production. India produces 107-110 million tonnes of wheat
annually. This bulletin covers variety selection, crop management, pest and disease control.

=======================================================================
SECTION 1: RECOMMENDED WHEAT VARIETIES — ZONE-WISE
=======================================================================

NORTH WESTERN PLAINS ZONE (Punjab, Haryana, Western UP, Rajasthan):
1. HD 3086 (Pusa Gautami):
   - Released: 2015 | Duration: 155–160 days
   - Yield: 55–60 q/ha (irrigated)
   - Resistant to Yellow Rust, Leaf Rust
   - Protein content: 12.0% | Chapati quality: Excellent
   
2. DBW 222 (Karan Narendra):
   - Released: 2019 | Duration: 152–157 days
   - Yield: 60–65 q/ha | BEST VARIETY CURRENTLY
   - Resistant to all three rusts (yellow, brown, stem)
   - Heat tolerant — good for late sowing
   
3. HD 2967:
   - Most widely grown variety in India
   - Yield: 50–55 q/ha | Duration: 155–160 days
   - Good chapati making quality
   - Susceptible to Yellow Rust — needs fungicide spray
   
4. PBW 723 (Punjab):
   - Yield: 55–60 q/ha
   - Excellent grain quality, bold grains
   - Recommended for Punjab only

NORTH EASTERN PLAINS ZONE (Eastern UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, W. Bengal):
1. HD 2781:
   - Yield: 45–50 q/ha | Late sown suitable
   
2. K 307 (Uttar Pradesh):
   - Yield: 40–45 q/ha | Drought tolerant
   - Suitable for rain-fed conditions

LATE SOWN (After 25 December) VARIETIES:
1. DBW 173 (Karan Vidya):
   - Designed for late sowing; short duration (130 days)
   - Yield: 40–45 q/ha even when sown in January
   
2. PBW 752:
   - Yield: 45–50 q/ha | Heat tolerant, late sowing

SECTION 2: SOWING SEASON AND PREPARATION
=======================================================================

OPTIMAL SOWING DATES:
| Zone                    | Timely Sowing     | Late Sowing      |
|------------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Punjab, Haryana        | 1–25 November     | After 10 Dec     |
| Western UP             | 1–25 November     | After 15 Dec     |
| Eastern UP, Bihar      | 15 Nov – 5 Dec    | After 20 Dec     |
| Rajasthan              | 1–25 November     | After 10 Dec     |
| Madhya Pradesh         | 15 Nov – 15 Dec   | After 25 Dec     |

Every week delay after optimal date = 35–40 kg/ha yield loss.

SEED RATE:
- Timely sown: 100 kg/ha (bold varieties) or 125 kg/ha (small grain)
- Late sown: 125 kg/ha (increased seed rate compensates for lost time)
- Raised beds: 80 kg/ha

SEED TREATMENT (mandatory):
1. Thiram 75% + Carboxin 37.5% (Vitavax): 2.5 g/kg seed
   - Prevents seed-borne diseases (Loose Smut, Karnal Bunt)
2. Imidacloprid 70% WS: 5 ml/kg seed — for aphid/shoot fly protection
3. Trichoderma viride: 5 g/kg seed — for soil-borne disease control

FIELD PREPARATION:
- Deep ploughing once in 3 years to break hard pan
- 2–3 discing + planking (to get fine seedbed)
- Apply FYM/compost: 10–15 t/ha as basal dressing

=======================================================================
SECTION 3: FERTILIZER MANAGEMENT
=======================================================================

RECOMMENDED DOSE (Irrigated, Timely Sown):
| Nutrient | Amount      | Application Timing               |
|---------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| N       | 120 kg/ha   | 1/3 basal + 1/3 at 1st irrigation + 1/3 at 2nd irr |
| P₂O₅   | 60 kg/ha    | Full basal (before sowing)        |
| K₂O    | 40 kg/ha    | Full basal                        |
| S       | 20 kg/ha    | Basal (as gypsum)                |
| Zn      | 25 kg/ha    | ZnSO₄ — basal every 3 years      |

PRACTICAL DOSES PER ACRE:
- Urea: 65 kg (basal 22 + 1st irrigation 22 + 2nd irrigation 21 kg)
- DAP: 55 kg (full basal)
- MOP: 25 kg (full basal)

LATE SOWN DOSE (reduced):
- N: 80 kg/ha | P: 40 kg/ha | K: 30 kg/ha

FOLIAR APPLICATION (for quality boost):
- 2% Urea spray at flowering + grain filling stage
- 0.5% ZnSO₄ spray at tillering if zinc deficiency (yellow bands on leaves)

ZERO TILLAGE WHEAT (after paddy):
- Saves Rs 2000–2500/acre in tillage cost
- No burning stubble — maintains soil health
- Same fertilizer dose; apply after sowing via top-dressing
- Use Furrow Irrigated Raised Bed (FIRB) for water saving

=======================================================================
SECTION 4: IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT
=======================================================================

Critical irrigation stages (6 total for optimal yield):
1. Crown Root Initiation (CRI): 20–22 DAS — MOST CRITICAL
2. Tillering: 40–45 DAS
3. Jointing/Stem Elongation: 60–65 DAS
4. Flowering/Anthesis: 85–90 DAS — SECOND MOST CRITICAL
5. Milk Stage: 100–105 DAS
6. Dough Stage: 115–120 DAS

WATER SAVING APPROACHES:
- Tensiometer-based irrigation (irrigate when soil moisture at 60 kPa)
- Bed and furrow irrigation (saves 25% water)
- Sprinkler irrigation (saves 30% water)
- Skip furrow method

SECTION 5: MAJOR DISEASES AND MANAGEMENT
=======================================================================

5.1 YELLOW RUST / STRIPE RUST (Puccinia striiformis)
Most important disease — can cause 40–70% yield loss in epidemics.

IDENTIFICATION:
- Yellow-orange pustules arranged in STRIPES along the leaf (distinct feature)
- Appears first on upper leaf surface
- Cool, moist weather (10–15°C) favours spread
- Most severe in Punjab, Himachal Pradesh foothills

MANAGEMENT:
- Grow resistant varieties: DBW 222, HD 3086
- Monitor from December onwards
- Propiconazole 25 EC: 0.1% spray (500 ml/200 litres water/acre)
- Tebuconazole 25.9% EC: 200 ml/acre spray
- Spray within 7 days of first observation — delayed spraying reduces effectiveness

5.2 BROWN RUST / LEAF RUST (Puccinia recondita)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Circular-oval orange pustules randomly scattered (NOT in stripes)
- Appears later than Yellow Rust (February–March)

MANAGEMENT:
- Propiconazole 25 EC: 200 ml/acre
- Spray at flag leaf emergence if >5% leaf area infected

5.3 STEM RUST (Puccinia graminis)
- Ug99 race — international threat, present in Karnataka hills
- Dark reddish-brown pustules on stem and leaf sheath

5.4 LOOSE SMUT (Ustilago tritici)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Entire ear replaced by black powdery mass (smut spores)
- Spreads via seed

MANAGEMENT:
- Hot Water Seed Treatment: soak seed 4 hours in water at 47°C
- Vitavax seed treatment (prevents 95% infection)

5.5 KARNAL BUNT (Tilletia indica)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Some grains in ear partially converted to black, foul-smelling mass
- Quarantine disease — affects marketability

MANAGEMENT:
- Propiconazole 25 EC: 200 ml/acre spray at ear emergence
- Use certified disease-free seed

5.6 POWDERY MILDEW (Blumeria graminis)
- White powdery patches on leaf surface
- Prevalent in cool, humid conditions
- Propiconazole or Triadimefon 25 WP: 200 g/acre

=======================================================================
SECTION 6: MAJOR PESTS
=======================================================================

6.1 APHIDS (Sitobion avenae, Rhopalosiphum padi)
ETL: 20 aphids per ear (flag leaf stage)

MANAGEMENT:
- Imidacloprid 17.8 SL: 150 ml/acre spray
- Thiamethoxam 25 WG: 40 g/acre
- Conserve natural enemies (ladybird beetles, parasitoid wasps)
- Avoid pre-anthesis insecticide sprays (to protect pollinators)

6.2 TERMITES (Odontotermes spp.)
- Severe in light sandy soils, dry seasons
MANAGEMENT:
- Chlorpyrifos 20 EC: 1 litre/acre drench in furrows at sowing

6.3 SHOOT FLY (Atherigona spp.)
- Young plants cut at base (deadheart symptom)
MANAGEMENT:
- Imidacloprid seed treatment prevents early-stage damage

=======================================================================
SECTION 7: HARVESTING AND THRESHING
=======================================================================

HARVEST TIMING:
- Hard dough stage — physiological maturity
- Grain moisture: 20–25% (at harvest), 12–14% (for storage)
- Harvesting during full ripeness → maximum yield

THRESHING:
- Combine harvesters (most efficient — 20–25 acres/day)
- Set concave clearance properly to reduce broken grains

STORAGE:
- Dry grain to 12% moisture before storage
- Use metal bins or CAP (Cover and Plinth) storage
- Aluminium phosphide (Celphos): 3 tablets per tonne for 7 days fumigation
