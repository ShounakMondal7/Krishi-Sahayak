ICAR TECHNICAL BULLETIN — Soybean Cultivation Guide for Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Rajasthan
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore, Madhya Pradesh
Document Reference: ICAR-IISR/Tech/2024/MP-MH-RJ-01

India is the world's 5th largest soybean producer. Madhya Pradesh alone contributes 55% of India's soybean
production. Maharashtra contributes 25%. Soybean has transformed the economy of central India farmers.
It is both a cash crop and a nitrogen fixer (fixes 40–80 kg N/ha), reducing next-crop fertilizer need.

=======================================================================
SECTION 1: RECOMMENDED VARIETIES
=======================================================================

MADHYA PRADESH (Main Zone):
1. JS 335 (Most Widely Grown):
   - Duration: 95–100 days | Yield: 20–25 q/ha
   - Upright plant, non-shattering, good for combine harvest
   - Purple flower | Yellow seed with yellow hilum
   
2. NRC 37 (Ahilya 3):
   - Duration: 95–100 days | Yield: 22–28 q/ha
   - Yellow mosaic resistant | Best for MP
   
3. JS 9305:
   - Duration: 90–95 days | Yield: 20–24 q/ha
   - Early maturing (good for late planting or double cropping)
   - Yellow Mosaic Virus resistant (important for MP)
   
4. RVS 2001-4:
   - High oil content (21.5%)
   - Suitable for black soils

MAHARASHTRA:
1. MAUS 71: Yield 18–22 q/ha
2. MAUS 162 (Phule Kalyani): Drought tolerant, Vidarbha
3. JS 335: Also grown in Maharashtra

RAJASTHAN:
1. NRC 7: Short duration (90 days)
2. JS 335: Most common

=======================================================================
SECTION 2: SOWING SEASON AND PREPARATION
=======================================================================

SOWING DATES:
- Optimal: 20 June – 10 July (onset of Kharif monsoon)
- Late sowing (after July 20): Yield reduction of 40–50 kg/ha per day
- Cannot sow before adequate soil moisture (first monsoon rain)

SEED RATE: 75 kg/ha (large seed) or 65 kg/ha (small seed)

SPACING:
- Between rows: 45 cm (for mechanized sowing) or 30 cm (for maximum yield)
- Between plants: 5–7 cm

SEED TREATMENT (Critical for nitrogen fixation):
1. Rhizobium japonicum inoculant: 200 g/10 kg seed
   - Mix with 250 ml 10% jaggery solution as sticker
   - Dry in shade for 30 minutes before sowing
   - NEVER mix with chemical seed treatment — kills Rhizobium
   
2. Phosphate Solubilising Bacteria (PSB): 200 g/10 kg seed
   
3. Chemical fungicide (apply separately, 2 days before Rhizobium):
   - Thiram 75 WS: 2 g/kg seed (seed-borne disease prevention)
   - Carbendazim 50 WP: 2 g/kg seed

FIELD PREPARATION:
- One deep ploughing + two disc harrowings
- Apply FYM 5 t/ha as basal

=======================================================================
SECTION 3: FERTILIZER MANAGEMENT
=======================================================================

Soybean is a legume — it fixes its own nitrogen via Rhizobium bacteria.
Chemical N requirement is very low if Rhizobium inoculation is done properly.

RECOMMENDED DOSE:
| Nutrient | Amount      | Notes                              |
|---------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| N       | 20 kg/ha    | Starter dose only (basal)         |
| P₂O₅   | 60–80 kg/ha | Basal — most important nutrient   |
| K₂O    | 40 kg/ha    | Basal                             |
| S       | 20 kg/ha    | As gypsum — basal                 |
| Zn      | 5 kg/ha     | ZnSO₄, if Zn deficiency           |
| Mo      | 1 g/kg seed | As ammonium molybdate — seed treatment |

MOLYBDENUM APPLICATION:
- Mo is essential for Rhizobium nodulation
- Seed treatment with 1 g Mo/kg seed increases yield by 10–15%
- Apply as sodium molybdate or ammonium molybdate solution

FOLIAR NUTRITION:
- DAP 2% spray at pod-fill stage: Increases seed size and oil content
- Boron 0.2% at flowering: Improves pod retention
- 2% K₂SO₄ spray at grain filling: Improves seed quality

=======================================================================
SECTION 4: MAJOR DISEASES OF SOYBEAN
=======================================================================

4.1 YELLOW MOSAIC VIRUS (YMV) — Most Serious
IDENTIFICATION:
- Bright yellow patches on leaves (mosaic pattern)
- Young leaves turn completely yellow in severe cases
- Stunted plant, very few pods
- Spread by whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)

MANAGEMENT:
- Grow YMV-resistant varieties: JS 9305, NRC 37, MAUS 71
- Control whitefly vector:
  - Dimethoate 30 EC: 1 litre/ha
  - Imidacloprid 17.8 SL: 500 ml/ha
- Remove and destroy infected plants in early stages
- No chemical treatment after infection — only prevention

4.2 STEM FLY (Melanagromyza sojae) — Major Pest and Disease
IDENTIFICATION:
- Maggots mine inside the stem (pith shows black discolouration)
- Plants wilt and die at seedling stage
- Dead plants pull out easily (roots intact)

MANAGEMENT:
- Carbofuran 3G: 30 kg/ha in furrows at sowing
- Triazophos 40 EC: 1 litre/acre spray at 15 and 25 DAS
- Imidacloprid seed treatment (reduces early damage)

4.3 BACTERIAL PUSTULE (Xanthomonas axonopodis)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Small, raised, yellow-brown pustules on leaf underside
- Yellowing and premature defoliation
- Worse after heavy rains followed by sunshine

MANAGEMENT:
- Copper oxychloride 0.3% spray
- Streptocycline + Copper oxychloride combination spray

4.4 CHARCOAL ROT (Macrophomina phaseolina)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Mid-season wilting (looks like drought stress)
- Stem base turns charcoal grey (fine black sclerotia visible in tissue)
- More common in dry years and sandy soils

MANAGEMENT:
- Crop rotation (avoid soybean-soybean)
- Soil application of T. viride at sowing
- Deep ploughing to bury sclerotia

4.5 FROGEYE LEAF SPOT (Cercospora sojina)
- Circular spots with grey centre, brown border
MANAGEMENT:
- Thiophanate methyl 70 WP: 0.1% spray

=======================================================================
SECTION 5: MAJOR PESTS
=======================================================================

5.1 GIRDLE BEETLE (Oberea brevis)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Beetle girdles (cuts a ring around) the stem at two points
- Upper part of plant wilts and breaks off
- Grub found inside the girdled portion

MANAGEMENT:
- Monocrotophos 36 SL: 750 ml/ha spray at early stage
- Pick and destroy beetles manually

5.2 SPODOPTERA LITURA (Tobacco Caterpillar)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Group feeding on leaves (skeletonization)
- Large brown caterpillars visible on leaves

MANAGEMENT:
- NPV spray (SNPV): 500 LE/ha — highly effective
- Chlorpyrifos 20 EC: 1 litre/acre
- Check for egg masses in field — crush early

5.3 LEAF FOLDER (Lamprosema indicate)
IDENTIFICATION:
- Leaves folded and webbed together
- Caterpillar feeds inside folded leaf

MANAGEMENT:
- Endosulfan 35 EC: 1.5 litres/ha (if permitted in state)
- Chlorpyrifos 20 EC: 1 litre/acre

=======================================================================
SECTION 6: HARVEST AND POST-HARVEST
=======================================================================

HARVEST INDICATORS:
- 90% pods have turned brown
- Moisture content 18–20%
- Seed moisture 14% (ideal for storage)

THRESHING:
- Combine harvester (most efficient)
- Cylinder speed: 600–700 RPM (higher speed = more seed damage)
- Sieve opening: 10–12 mm

POST-HARVEST PROCESSING:
- Dry to 12% moisture (sun-drying 2–3 days)
- Clean seed — remove damaged, shrivelled seeds
- Store in moisture-proof bags

MARKET PRICES (2024-25):
- MSP: ₹4,892/quintal
- Typical market price: ₹4,500–5,500/quintal depending on quality
- Oil content >18% gets premium price

VALUE ADDITION:
- Soy milk, tofu — village-level processing
- Soy flour — profitable small-scale enterprise
- Soybean oil extraction (large scale)
