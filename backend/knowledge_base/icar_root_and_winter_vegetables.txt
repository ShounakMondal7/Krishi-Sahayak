ICAR COMPREHENSIVE PRODUCTION & AGRONOMY GUIDE: ROOT CROPS & WINTER VEGETABLES
INDIAN COUNCIL OF AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH (ICAR-IARI) EXTENSION BULLETIN

1. CARROT CULTIVATION (Daucus carota var. sativus — Gajar)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Soil, Climate & Sowing:
- Soil: Deep, well-drained, loose sandy loam to loamy soil rich in organic matter. Soil pH 6.0 - 7.0 is optimal. Compact, heavy clay or gravelly soils cause root forking, branching, and cracking, severely reducing Grade-A marketability.
- Climate: Cool-season crop. Optimal germination temperature is 18°C - 24°C; optimal root coloration and sweetness develop at 15°C - 20°C. High temperatures (>28°C) result in pale color, tough texture, and high fiber.
- Sowing Time:
  * Northern Plains (UP, Haryana, Punjab, Bihar): Asiatic types from August to October; European (temperate) types from October to November.
  * Sowing Method: Raised beds (ridges 45 cm apart, 20-25 cm high) ensure straight, clean, unforked roots. Seed rate: 4-5 kg/ha for Asiatic varieties, 6-7 kg/ha for European types. Thin seedlings to 6-8 cm spacing at 15-20 days after germination.

B. High-Yielding ICAR Varieties:
- Pusa Rudhira (ICAR-IARI): Red Asiatic variety, rich in lycopene (7.5 mg/100g) and antioxidants. Long, straight roots (25-30 cm), self-colored core, maturity in 85-90 days, yield 300-350 q/ha. Excellent for fresh market salad and cooking.
- Pusa Asita: Black/deep purple Asiatic variety, extraordinarily rich in anthocyanins (superfood category). Self-colored core, roots 22-25 cm, yield 250-300 q/ha. High industrial and pharmaceutical market demand.
- Pusa Meghali: Orange Asiatic x European derivative, rich in beta-carotene (provitamin A). Self-colored orange core, early maturing (100-110 days), yield 250-300 q/ha.
- Nantes Half Long: European type, cylindrical blunt-tipped orange roots (15-18 cm), sweet tender flesh, very small core. Ideal for gourmet and salad markets.
- Pusa Kesar: Asiatic red variety, stay-green foliage, less root forking, maturity in 90-100 days.

C. Fertilizer & Nutrient Management:
- FYM / Compost: 20-25 tonnes/ha well-decomposed FYM incorporated 4 weeks before sowing. Avoid fresh unrotted manure as it causes root forking and secondary root proliferation.
- NPK Dosage: 60 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, and 80 kg K2O per hectare. Potassium is vital for root carotene synthesis, crispness, and cold storage longevity.
- Application: Apply entire P, K, and 50% N as basal dose in furrows. Top dress remaining 50% N in two equal splits at 30 and 45 days after sowing during earthing up.

D. Major Diseases & Pest Management:
- Alternaria Leaf Blight (Alternaria dauci):
  * Symptoms: Small dark-brown to black irregular spots on leaf margins with yellow halos; older leaves appear scorched or burned, leading to complete defoliation and poor root enlargement.
  * Organic/Bio-control: Seed treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 4 g/kg seed. Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or copper hydroxide @ 2 g/L at first symptom.
  * Verified Chemical Control: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.0 g/L (30 g per 15-L knapsack pump) or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 mL/L (15 mL per 15-L pump) at 10-14 day intervals.
  * Safety: Adhere strictly to 10-day Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI).
- Powdery Mildew (Erysiphe heraclei):
  * Symptoms: White talcum-like powdery growth on upper leaf surfaces and petioles in dry, cool conditions.
  * Control: Foliar spray of Wettable Sulphur 80 WP @ 2.5 g/L or Hexaconazole 5 EC @ 1 mL/L.
- Root-Knot Nematode (Meloidogyne incognita):
  * Symptoms: Stunted growth, root galling, severe root forking, and hairy taproots.
  * Management: Deep summer plowing; apply Carbofuran 3G @ 20 kg/ha or Neem cake @ 2.5 q/ha during land preparation.

E. Harvesting, Grading & Post-Harvest Storage:
- Harvesting: Roots reach commercial maturity when diameter reaches 2.5-3.5 cm. Irrigate lightly 24 hours prior to pulling to facilitate smooth manual pulling without root snapping.
- Washing & Hydro-cooling: Wash immediately in clean running water to dislodge soil clods and reduce core temperature.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A (Export/Supermarket): >15 cm long, straight, uniform taper, no blemishes, no green shoulders, washed clean.
  * Grade B (Standard APMC): 10-15 cm, minor curvature.
  * Grade C (Reject/Processing): <10 cm, forked, cracked, or mechanical gouges.
- Packaging: 40-50 kg perforated HDPE leno mesh bags.
- Cold Storage: 0°C to 1°C at 95-98% RH provides 4 to 6 months safe storage life. Never store near ethylene-generating apples, pears, or melons.

2. RADISH AGRONOMY & PROTECTION (Raphanus sativus — Mooli)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Varieties & Sowing:
- Varieties: Pusa Chetki (heat tolerant, sow April-August, 40-45 days), Pusa Desi (winter white root, 50-55 days), Japanese White (snow-white, cylindrical, 25-30 cm, very sweet and tender), Arka Nishant (resistant to root splitting and pithiness).
- Sowing: Raised ridges 45 cm apart; seed rate 8-10 kg/ha.

B. Integrated Pest & Disease Management:
- Mustard Sawfly & Aphids: Spray Malathion 50 EC @ 1.5 mL/L or Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 3 mL/L.
- White Rust (Albugo candida): White porcelain-like blisters on underside of leaves; spray Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold) @ 2.0 g/L.

C. Post-Harvest:
- Wash thoroughly, tie in 10-12 root bundles with fresh green foliage for quick mandi dispatch. Sell within 24-48 hours or hold at 0°C with 95% RH for up to 1-2 weeks.

3. GREEN PEAS CULTIVATION (Pisum sativum — Hari Matar)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. High Yielding ICAR Varieties:
- Early Varieties (maturity 55-65 days): Arkel, Azad Pea-1, Kashi Nandini, Pusa Pragati (resistant to powdery mildew).
- Mid & Late Varieties (maturity 80-110 days): Bonneville, Azad Pea-3, Pusa Shree.
- Seed Rate & Rhizobium Inoculation: 80-100 kg/ha for early varieties, 60-75 kg/ha for mid varieties. Treat seed with Rhizobium leguminosarum culture @ 20 g/kg seed to fix atmospheric nitrogen.

B. Diseases & Crop Protection:
- Powdery Mildew (Erysiphe pisi):
  * Major limiting disease. White patches spread over entire leaf surface, stems, and pods.
  * Control: Spray Wettable Sulphur 80 WP @ 2.5 g/L or Dinocap 48 EC @ 1 mL/L or Azoxystrobin 23 SC @ 1 mL/L.
- Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera):
  * Control: Install pheromone traps @ 10/ha; spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 mL/L (4.5 mL per 15-L pump) at flower drop and pod set.

C. Harvesting & Storage:
- Pick when pods are fully filled and swollen but seeds remain tender and sweet. Sugar rapidly converts to starch within 24 hours at ambient temperature. Hydro-cool and store at 0°C with 95% RH.

4. CAULIFLOWER & CABBAGE (Cole Crops)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Varieties:
- Cauliflower: Pusa Deepali (early), Pusa Sharad (mid-early), Pusa Snowball K-1 (late).
- Cabbage: Golden Acre, Pride of India, Pusa Mukta (black rot resistant).

B. Physiological Disorders & Deficiencies:
- Whiptail in Cauliflower: Molybdenum deficiency on acid soils (pH < 5.5). Leaves become narrow and strap-like. Correct by foliar spray of Ammonium Molybdate @ 1 g/L.
- Browning / Hollow Stem: Boron deficiency. Curds show water-soaked browning. Apply Borax @ 10-15 kg/ha to soil or foliar spray of Solubor @ 1.5 g/L.
- Buttoning: Formation of tiny premature buttons due to poor nitrogen, overdue seedlings, or temperature shock.

C. Pest Management:
- Diamondback Moth (DBM - Plutella xylostella):
  * Resistant pest. Use mustard as trap crop (1 row mustard after every 25 rows cabbage/cauliflower).
  * Bio-control: Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) kurstaki @ 1.5 g/L or Spinosad 45 SC @ 0.3 mL/L.
