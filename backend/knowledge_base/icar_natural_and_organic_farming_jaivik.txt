SECTION 1: CORE PILLARS OF NATURAL FARMING (ZBNF / BPKP)
=======================================================================
1. Philosophy & Basic Principles:
   - Eliminates all purchased synthetic fertilizers and chemical pesticides.
   - Relies on native indigenous cow (Desi cow: Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Tharparkar, Rathi) dung and urine containing beneficial microbial consortia.
   - Four Core Pillars: Beejamrit (Seed treatment), Jeevamrit (Soil inoculant), Mulching / Achhadan (Soil moisture & microbes), and Waaphasa (Moisture-air balance).

=======================================================================
SECTION 2: STEP-BY-STEP PREPARATION OF ON-FARM BIO-FORMULATIONS
=======================================================================
1. Jeevamrit (Liquid Microbial Culture for 1 Acre):
   - Ingredients:
     * Fresh indigenous cow dung: 10 kg
     * Indigenous cow urine: 5 to 10 liters
     * Organic jaggery (Gud) or sugarcane juice: 1 to 2 kg (carbon food for microbes)
     * Pulse flour (Besan - Gram/Pigeon pea): 1 to 2 kg (protein source)
     * Virgin forest or uncultivated field-bund soil: 1 handful (100–200 g) rich in native bacteria
     * Clean non-chlorinated water: 200 liters
   - Preparation Method:
     * Mix dung, urine, jaggery, flour, and soil in a 200-liter plastic drum containing 200L water.
     * Stir clockwise with a wooden stick twice daily (morning and evening for 2 minutes).
     * Keep the drum under tree shade covered with a breathable jute bag.
     * Ferment for 48 to 72 hours in summer (4 to 6 days in winter).
   - Application & Dosage:
     * Soil Drenching / Flood Irrigation: Apply 200 liters per acre along with irrigation water every 15 to 21 days.
     * Foliar Spray: Filter through a two-fold khadi cloth and dilute 10% (1.5 liters of Jeevamrit per 15-Liter knapsack pump with water).

2. Beejamrit (Protective Seed & Seedling Treatment):
   - Ingredients: 5 kg cow dung, 5 liters cow urine, 50 g slaked lime (Chuna), 1 handful native bund soil, 20 liters water.
   - Preparation: Tie dung in cloth and submerge in water for 12 hours. Squeeze dung extract, add urine, lime water, and bund soil. Stir well.
   - Dosage: Coat seeds gently before sowing (air dry in shade for 30 minutes). For transplanted crops (Paddy, Tomato, Chilli), dip roots for 5 minutes. Protects against seedling rot and collar rot.

3. Ghanajeevamrit (Solid Dry Bio-Fertilizer for Basal Dressing):
   - Ingredients: 100 kg dry indigenous cow dung, 5 liters cow urine, 1 kg jaggery, 1 kg besan, handful fertile soil.
   - Preparation: Mix ingredients thoroughly on clean polythene sheet, sprinkle with urine-jaggery solution, form into small heap and cover with gunny bag for 48 hours. Spread in shade to dry and store.
   - Dosage: Apply 100 to 200 kg per acre as basal soil dressing during final land preparation before sowing.

SECTION 3: BOTANICAL BIOCIDES & NATURAL PEST DEFENCE
=======================================================================
1. Neemastra (Against Sucking Pests — Aphids, Whiteflies, Jassids):
   - Formula: 5 kg crushed neem leaves or 5 kg fresh neem seed kernel powder + 5 liters cow urine + 2 kg fresh cow dung + 100 liters water.
   - Ferment for 48 hours in shade. Filter well.
   - Dosage: Spray directly at 100% strength or 1.5 L per 15-Liter knapsack pump. No chemical dilution needed.

2. Brahmastra (Against Leaf Eating Caterpillars & Pod Borers):
   - Formula: 10 liters cow urine + 3 kg crushed neem leaves + 2 kg custard apple leaves + 2 kg papaya leaves + 2 kg pomegranate leaves + 2 kg guava/castor leaves.
   - Boil the mixture on low flame until volume reduces by half. Cool for 24 hours, filter and store in bottles (stable for 6 months).
   - Dosage: Dilute 300–400 ml of Brahmastra per 15-Liter knapsack spray pump (20–25 ml per liter of water).

3. Agniastra (Against Resistant Borers & Hard-Shelled Sucking Pests):
   - Formula: 10 liters cow urine + 500 g hot green chilli paste + 500 g crushed garlic paste + 500 g neem leaf paste + 250 g tobacco leaf powder.
   - Boil on gentle heat for 30 minutes. Cool for 48 hours. Filter carefully.
   - Dosage: Dilute 250 to 300 ml of Agniastra per 15-Liter knapsack pump (15–20 ml/L).

4. Dashaparni Ark (Broad-Spectrum Botanical Anti-Fungal & Insecticide):
   - Formula: 200 liters water + 20 liters cow urine + 2 kg cow dung + 200 g turmeric + 500 g ginger + 10 types of local bitter/astringent leaves (2 kg each: Neem, Karanj, Castor, Custard apple, Datura, Bel, Calotropis/Aak, Guava, Lantana, Marigold).
   - Ferment in shade for 30 to 40 days. Stir every alternate day.
   - Dosage: 500 ml extract per 15-Liter knapsack pump. Controls both insect pests and early blights.

SECTION 4: VERMICOMPOSTING & BIO-FERTILIZERS
=======================================================================
1. Scientific Vermicomposting:
   - Species: Eisenia fetida (African earthworm) or Eudrilus eugeniae.
   - Bed Size: 10 ft length × 3 ft width × 2 ft height. Provide 50% shade with thatched roof.
   - Layering: Base layer 3 inches crushed twigs/straw for drainage; next layer 6 inches pre-decomposed farm waste; top layer 3 inches semi-rotted cow dung.
   - Moisture & Temperature: Maintain 60-70% moisture (squeeze a handful; droplets should appear without dripping) and 25-30°C temperature.
   - Harvest: Ready in 45-60 days when top looks like granular dark brown tea powder. Stop watering 3 days before harvesting.
   - Application: 1 to 2 tonnes per acre for field crops; 5 kg per tree basin for fruit orchards.

2. Azolla Microphylla Bio-Fertilizer:
   - Rapidly doubles biomass in 3-5 days in 2-meter polythene pits with 10 cm standing water.
   - Fixes 20-40 kg atmospheric nitrogen per hectare when grown as dual crop with wet season paddy.
   - Excellent supplemental green feed for cattle and poultry (contains 22-25% crude protein).

SECTION 5: PKVY & PGS-INDIA ORGANIC CERTIFICATION
=======================================================================
1. Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) Scheme Benefits:
   - Financial Assistance: ₹50,000 per hectare over 3 years.
   - ₹31,000/ha transferred directly to farmer for organic inputs (bio-fertilizers, vermicompost, botanical formulations).
   - ₹8,800/ha for value addition, packaging, branding, and local transport.
   - Cluster Requirement: Formed in clusters of 20 or more farmers covering a minimum of 20 hectares (50 acres).

2. PGS-India (Participatory Guarantee System) Certification Process:
   - Peer-review certification operated directly by farmer groups (Local Groups of 5 or more farmers).
   - Conversion Timeline:
     * Year 1: PGS-Green (Under Conversion - Year 1).
     * Year 2: PGS-Green (Under Conversion - Year 2).
     * Year 3: PGS-Green (Under Conversion - Year 3).
     * After 36 months of organic practice: Full PGS-India Organic (Green Logo with verified organic status).
   - Web Portal: Free peer-review registration on pgsindia-ncof.gov.in.
