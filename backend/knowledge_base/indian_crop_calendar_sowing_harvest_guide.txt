INDIAN CROP CALENDAR, AGRO-CLIMATIC SOWING & HARVESTING GUIDE
================================================================================
Comprehensive Month-by-Month ICAR Production Calendar for 70+ Field & Horticultural Crops.

SECTION 1: THE THREE INDIAN CROPPING SEASONS & MONSOON DYNAMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Kharif Season (South-West Monsoon Crops):
   - Sowing Window: June 15 to July 15 (onset of SW monsoon). Late sown up to August 10 in delayed monsoon zones.
   - Harvesting: October to November.
   - Major Crops: Paddy (Rice), Maize, Cotton, Soybean, Groundnut, Pigeon pea (Tur/Arhar), Green gram (Moong), Black gram (Urad), Pearl millet (Bajra), Sorghum (Jowar), Sesame (Til), Jute.
2. Rabi Season (Winter / Post-Monsoon Crops):
   - Sowing Window: October 15 to November 30 (as temperature drops below 25°C).
   - Harvesting: March to April (before onset of severe dry summer).
   - Major Crops: Wheat, Barley, Mustard / Rapeseed, Chickpea (Chana), Field Pea (Matar), Lentil (Masoor), Potato, Onion (Rabi), Garlic, Cumin (Jeera), Coriander, Isabgol.
3. Zaid / Summer Season (Pre-Monsoon Irrigated Crops):
   - Sowing Window: February 15 to March 25 (utilizing residual soil moisture and assured irrigation).
   - Harvesting: May to June.
   - Major Crops: Summer Moong (Short duration 60-day varieties), Summer Urad, Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Bottle Gourd, Bitter Gourd, Okra (Bhindi), Fodder Maize, Fodder Sorghum/Bajra.

SECTION 2: MONTH-BY-MONTH SOWING & MANAGEMENT CALENDAR (PAN-INDIA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- JANUARY:
  * Sowing: Late-sown wheat (PBW 373, DBW 173) in North India; Summer Groundnut in Gujarat/Andhra Pradesh; Summer Sesamum.
  * Nursery Sowing: Summer vegetables (Tomato, Chilli, Brinjal, Capsicum in pro-trays); Watermelon and Muskmelon on plastic mulch in South and West India.
  * Harvesting: Sugarcane (Autumn planted), Mustard (early toria varieties), Potato (early crop), Cotton (last picking).
  * Critical Irrigation: Wheat (Late jointing / Booting stage, 65-70 DAS).

- FEBRUARY:
  * Sowing: Sunflower (KBSH 53, PAC 36), Summer Moong (Pusa Vishal, SML 668), Summer Urad (PU 31), Summer Bajra, Spring Maize, Okra (Arka Anamika, Pusa Sawani).
  * Direct Seeding: Cucurbits (Cucumber, Pumpkin, Ridge gourd, Bitter gourd) in riverbeds and raised beds.
  * Harvesting: Rapeseed (Toria), Early Chickpea, Sugar beet, Cabbage, Cauliflower.

- MARCH:
  * Sowing: Summer Moong (last window up to March 20), Cowpea (Lobia), Sugarcane (Spring planting - Co 0238, CoLk 94184, Co 86032), Jute (JRO 524, JBO 2003) in West Bengal/Assam.
  * Harvesting: Rabi Mustard (when 75-80% pods turn golden yellow), Rabi Pulses (Chana, Masoor), Cumin, Coriander, Rabi Potato.
  * Irrigation: Terminal irrigation in Wheat at soft dough stage (preventing premature desiccation during March heat spikes).

- APRIL:
  * Sowing: Green manure crops (Dhaincha / Sesbania aculeata @ 20 kg/acre, Sunnhemp) in fields scheduled for Kharif Paddy; Fodder Sorghum (SSG 59-3).
  * Harvesting: Wheat (harvesting using Combine Harvester or Reaper at 12-14% grain moisture), Barley, Lentil, Chickpea.
  * Field Operation: Deep summer plowing (MB plow) to expose resting pupae of pod borers and root-knot nematode cysts to scorching 42°C+ solar heat.

- MAY:
  * Sowing: Pre-monsoon Cotton (in irrigated Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan); Nursery raising for Long-duration Kharif Paddy (Swarna, Samba Mahsuri, CR Dhan 307) from May 20-31.
  * Land Preparation: Incorporation of green manure (Dhaincha) into soil after 45-50 days of growth (before flowering) using disc harrow.
  * Harvesting: Summer Moong (harvest pods in morning to prevent pod shattering), Summer vegetables, Onion (Rabi crop curing and storage).

- JUNE:
  * Sowing: Kharif Nursery Sowing: Basmati Rice (Pusa Basmati 1121, 1509, 1718, 1847), Medium Paddy (Pusa 44, MTU 1010).
  * Direct Sowing (with onset of monsoon): Soybean (JS 20-34, JS 20-98, NRC 127), Groundnut (TG 37A, Kadiri 6, GJG 9), Cotton, Maize (DHM 117, PMH 1), Arhar/Tur (ICPH 2740, Pusa 992).
  * Harvesting: Summer Sesame, Summer Fodder crops.

- JULY:
  * Sowing: Main Transplanting Window for Paddy (21 to 25-day old seedlings at 20 cm x 15 cm spacing, 2 seedlings per hill).
  * Direct Sowing: Bajra (HHB 67 Improved, MPMH 17), Jowar, Kharif Pulses (Moong, Urad, Cowpea), Sesame, Castor.
  * Weeding: Pre-emergence herbicide sprays (Pendimethalin, Pretilachlor) within 48-72 hours of sowing/transplanting.

- AUGUST:
  * Sowing: Late-kharif onion nursery, Toria (Brassica rapa var. toria - TL 15, PT 303), Early Cauliflower.
  * Management: Top dressing of Nitrogen (Urea) in Paddy at active tillering (21-25 DAT) and panicle initiation (42-45 DAT); Earthing-up in Maize and Sugarcane.
  * Pest Scouting: Monitor for Paddy Stem Borer (dead hearts), Brown Planthopper (BPH), Cotton Pink Bollworm, Fall Armyworm (FAW) in Maize.

- SEPTEMBER:
  * Sowing: Toria (early mustard catch crop), Early Potato (Kufri Pukhraj, Kufri Surya), Early Green Peas (Arkel, Matar Ageta 6), Radish, Carrot, Spinach.
  * Harvesting: Kharif Moong, Urad, Short-duration Maize, Early Bajra.
  * Field Sanitation: Drain excess floodwater and prevent water stagnation after heavy late monsoon downpours.

- OCTOBER:
  * Sowing: Main Rabi Sowing Season: Mustard (Pusa Bold, RH 749, Giriraj, Kranti) from Oct 10 to Oct 25; Chickpea (JG 14, JAKI 9218, RVG 202); Lentil (IPL 81, HUL 57); Rabi Onion Nursery.
  * Sowing: Autumn Sugarcane (Co 0238) intercropped with Potato or Mustard.
  * Harvesting: Kharif Paddy (Swarna, Hybrid 6444), Soybean, Groundnut, Cotton (first flush), Kharif Maize.

- NOVEMBER:
  * Sowing: Timely Sown Irrigated Wheat (Nov 1 to Nov 25) - High Yielding Varieties: HD 2967, HD 3086, DBW 187 (Karan Vandana), DBW 222 (Karan Narendra), DBW 303; Rabi Maize, Garlic, Coriander.
  * Harvesting: Basmati Rice (Pusa 1121, 1509), Pigeon Pea (early varieties), Cotton (second picking).
  * Critical Irrigation: First irrigation in Wheat at 21 DAS (Crown Root Initiation - CRI stage). Missing this irrigation reduces tillering by 25%!

- DECEMBER:
  * Sowing: Late-sown Wheat (up to Dec 25 - PBW 373, HD 3059, DBW 173) with 25% higher seed rate (50 kg/acre).
  * Transplanting: Rabi Onion seedlings (45-50 days old) on flat beds or broad beds at 15 cm x 10 cm spacing.
  * Frost Protection: Evening light irrigation and smoke blankets in North-West India when minimum temperature falls below 4°C.
  * Harvesting: Late Kharif Paddy, Autumn Sugarcane, Toria, Early Peas.
