MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE & FARMERS WELFARE / AGMARKNET / E-NAM / CACP STANDARDS

1. OVERVIEW OF INDIAN AGRICULTURAL COMMODITY PRICING ARCHITECTURE
Agricultural commodities in India fall under two distinct price-discovery and market mechanisms:

A. Central Government MSP (Minimum Support Price) Mandated Crops:
- The Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) recommends MSP for 23 mandated crops based on comprehensive cost of cultivation (A2+FL formula providing at least 50% margin over production costs).
- Mandatory MSP crops include:
  * Cereals: Paddy / Rice, Wheat, Maize, Jowar (Sorghum), Bajra (Pearl Millet), Ragi (Finger Millet), Barley (Jau).
  * Pulses: Gram / Chickpea (Chana), Tur / Arhar (Pigeon Pea), Moong (Green Gram), Urad (Black Gram), Lentil (Masoor).
  * Oilseeds: Groundnut (Peanut), Rapeseed & Mustard (Sarson), Soybean, Sunflower seed, Sesamum (Til), Safflower (Kusum), Nigerseed.
  * Commercial Crops: Cotton (Kapas - Medium and Long Staple), Sugarcane (FRP - Fair & Remunerative Price fixed by CCEA), Raw Jute, Copra (Coconut).
- Procurement Agencies: Food Corporation of India (FCI), NAFED, Cotton Corporation of India (CCI), Jute Corporation of India (JCI), State Civil Supplies Corporations.
- Procurement Standards: Strict adherence to Fair Average Quality (FAQ) parameters (moisture thresholds typically <=12-14%, refractions/foreign matter <=1%, insect-damaged kernels <=1-3%). Produce failing FAQ standards is rejected or subjected to official dockage deductions.

B. Non-MSP Horticultural & Perishable Crops (Operation Greens / APMC Mandis):
- Perishable vegetables, fruits, and spices do NOT receive central MSP procurement guarantees. Prices fluctuate daily based on supply-demand dynamics, arrivals in APMC (Agricultural Produce Market Committee) terminal mandis, seasonal flushes, transit bottlenecks, and cold-chain infrastructure.
- Traded via open physical outcry auctions, electronic bidding on e-NAM (National Agriculture Market), and contract direct-purchase agreements.
- Government market stabilization occurs via Operation Greens (Ministry of Food Processing Industries), Market Intervention Scheme (MIS), and NAFED price stabilization buffer funds for TOP (Tomato, Onion, Potato) and 22 perishables (TOTAL crops).

2. ROOT & TUBER VEGETABLES — APMC WHOLESALE MANDI BENCHMARKS (2025-26)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Carrot (Daucus carota — Gajar):
- Market Mechanism: Open APMC Mandi Wholesale Auction; daily trading at regional mandis (Azadpur, Dadri Mandi, Hapur, Meerut, Agra, Alwar).
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,500 - ₹2,200 per quintal (₹15 - ₹22 per kg wholesale).
- Farmgate & Local Primary Yard Rate: ₹12 - ₹17 per kg.
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹30 - ₹40 per kg.
- Quality Grading Standards:
  * Grade A (Top Premium): Long, straight, tapered taproots >15 cm, bright uniform red/orange color, smooth surface, crack-free, washed and free of field soil clods.
  * Grade B (Fair Average): Medium length 10-15 cm, slight curvature permitted, uniform thickness.
  * Grade C (Reject / Processing): Forked, cracked, crooked, woody roots, or undersized (<10 cm).
- Post-Harvest Handling & Packaging:
  * Hydro-cooling immediately after harvest in cool water lowers field heat and preserves crispness.
  * Pack in 40-50 kg breathable HDPE leno mesh bags or ventilated plastic crates.
- Storage & Holding Parameters:
  * Cold storage temperature: 0°C to 1°C with 95% to 98% Relative Humidity (RH).
  * Shelf-life: 4 to 6 months in commercial cold storage.
  * CRITICAL: Never store carrots in the same cold chamber with ethylene-emitting fruits (such as apples, pears, peaches) as ethylene triggers isocoumarin development, causing a bitter taste.

B. Radish (Raphanus sativus — Mooli):
- Market Mechanism: Open APMC Mandi Wholesale Auction.
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹800 - ₹1,400 per quintal (₹8 - ₹14 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹18 - ₹25 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Crisp, tender, straight white roots >20 cm, diameter 3-4 cm, solid pith-free flesh, sweet pungency.
  * Grade B: Medium roots 15-20 cm.
  * Grade C: Spongy/pithy roots, forked, or bolted.
- Packaging & Storage:
  * Wash clean of soil; bundle in units of 10-12 with foliage intact for daily local markets; decapitate tops for distant transport.
  * Highly perishable; hold at 0°C with 95% RH for maximum 1-2 weeks.

C. Beetroot (Beta vulgaris — Chukandar):
- Market Mechanism: Open APMC Mandi Wholesale Auction.
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,400 - ₹2,100 per quintal (₹14 - ₹21 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹30 - ₹40 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Uniform round globe shape 5-7 cm diameter, smooth skin, deep blood-red interior rings, fiber-free.
  * Grade B: Medium 4-5 cm.
  * Grade C: Oversized (>8 cm, woody core), deformed, or cracked roots.
- Storage: Trim green tops leaving 2 cm neck; hold at 0°C to 2°C with 95% RH for 3 to 5 months.

D. Potato (Solanum tuberosum — Aloo):
- Market Mechanism: Open APMC Wholesale Mandi Auction; cold store warehouse receipt finance; NAFED market intervention during glut.
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,350 - ₹1,750 per quintal (₹14 - ₹18 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹22 - ₹28 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Large clean tubers >45 mm diameter, well-cured intact skin, green-free, hollow-heart free.
  * Grade B: Medium table grade 30-45 mm diameter.
  * Grade C: Small/seed tubers <30 mm (chhatta) or mechanical cuts/blighted tubers.
- Storage: Cold store at 3°C to 4°C with 90-95% RH for table potatoes in 50 kg leno mesh bags. Processing varieties (Kufri Chipsona) stored at 8-10°C with CIPC sprout suppressant.

3. COLE CROPS, LEAFY GREENS & FRUIT VEGETABLES — APMC WHOLESALE BENCHMARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Cauliflower (Brassica oleracea var. botrytis — Phool Gobhi):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,000 - ₹1,700 per quintal (₹10 - ₹17 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹22 - ₹30 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Compact, firm, snow-white curds >15 cm diameter, jacket leaves attached, blemish and borer-free.
  * Grade B: Creamy-white, medium curds 10-15 cm.
  * Grade C: Loose, ricey, browning, or insect-damaged curds.
- Storage: 0°C with 95-98% RH; shelf life 2-3 weeks.

B. Cabbage (Brassica oleracea var. capitata — Patta Gobhi):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹800 - ₹1,300 per quintal (₹8 - ₹13 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹18 - ₹25 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Solid, heavy, firm heads 1.0 to 1.8 kg, tight wrapper leaves, crack-free.
  * Grade B: Heads <1.0 kg or slightly loose.
  * Grade C: Burst heads, Caterpillar-bored heads.
- Storage: 0°C with 95% RH; shelf life 6-8 weeks.

C. Green Peas (Pisum sativum — Hari Matar):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹2,200 - ₹3,600 per quintal (₹22 - ₹36 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹45 - ₹65 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Well-filled, bright green plump pods, 8-10 sweet tender seeds per pod, fiberless.
  * Grade B: Moderately filled pods (5-7 seeds).
  * Grade C: Flat pods, yellowing, starchy, over-mature or weevil-damaged pods.
- Post-Harvest Handling: Hydro-cooling preserves sucrose; store at 0°C with 95% RH for 1-2 weeks.

D. Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum — Tamatar):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,200 - ₹1,800 per quintal (₹12 - ₹18 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹25 - ₹35 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Firm, smooth, uniform red (turning/breaker stage for transit), blemish-free, >55 mm diameter.
  * Grade B: Medium size 40-55 mm.
  * Grade C: Soft, overripe, misshapen, blotchy ripening, or cracked.
- Storage: Breaker stage at 12-15°C with 85-90% RH; do not refrigerate below 10°C to avoid chilling injury.

E. Onion (Allium cepa — Pyaaz):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,800 - ₹2,400 per quintal (₹18 - ₹24 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹28 - ₹38 per kg.
- Quality Grading:
  * Grade A: Large uniform globes >55 mm, dry tight papery skins, thin neck, well-cured.
  * Grade B: Medium 40-55 mm.
  * Grade C: Small/chhanta (<40 mm), thick-neck (bolters), sprouted or double bulbs.
- Storage: Well-ventilated traditional kanda chawls with bottom aeration at 25-30°C and 65-70% RH; avoid dampness.

F. Green Chilli (Capsicum annuum — Hari Mirch):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹2,500 - ₹3,800 per quintal (₹25 - ₹38 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹45 - ₹65 per kg.
- Quality Grading: Grade A: Crisp, dark green, 7-10 cm, intact green calyx stalk, anthracnose-free.
- Storage: 7-10°C with 90-95% RH for 2-3 weeks.

G. Garlic (Allium sativum — Lahsun):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹8,500 - ₹13,000 per quintal (₹85 - ₹130 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹150 - ₹200 per kg.
- Quality Grading: Grade A: Bulbs >45 mm diameter, 10-15 compact plump cloves, clean dry white skins.
- Storage: 0-2°C with 60-70% RH in aerated racks.

H. Fresh Ginger (Zingiber officinale — Adrak):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹4,800 - ₹7,000 per quintal (₹48 - ₹70 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹80 - ₹110 per kg.
- Quality Grading: Grade A: Bold, plump rhizomes, washed clean of mud, skin healed, fiber-free.
- Storage: 12-14°C with 85-90% RH.

I. Okra / Ladyfinger (Abelmoschus esculentus — Bhindi):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,700 - ₹2,800 per quintal (₹17 - ₹28 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹35 - ₹50 per kg.
- Quality Grading: Grade A: Bright green tender pods 7-10 cm, crisp snapping tips, borer-free.
- Storage: 7-10°C with 90-95% RH for 7-10 days.

J. Brinjal / Eggplant (Solanum melongena — Baingan):
- Modal APMC Wholesale Rate: ₹1,100 - ₹1,800 per quintal (₹11 - ₹18 per kg wholesale).
- Retail Consumer Range: ₹25 - ₹35 per kg.
- Quality Grading: Grade A: Glossy purple/green skin, tender calyx, seedless, shoot-and-fruit borer free.
- Storage: 10-12°C with 90% RH for 1-2 weeks.

4. OFFICIAL MSP CROPS — GOVERNMENT OF INDIA RATES & PROCUREMENT SPECS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Wheat (Triticum aestivum):
- Central MSP Rate: ₹2,425 per quintal.
- Procurement Window: April - June (Rabi Marketing Season).
- FAQ Quality Specifications: Moisture <= 12%, Foreign matter <= 0.75%, Other foodgrains <= 2.0%, Damaged grains <= 2.0%, Slightly damaged <= 4.0%, Shriveled/broken <= 6.0%.

B. Paddy / Rice (Oryza sativa):
- Central MSP Rate: Common Grade: ₹2,300 per quintal; Grade A: ₹2,320 per quintal.
- Procurement Window: October - March (Kharif Marketing Season).
- FAQ Quality Specifications: Moisture <= 14%, Foreign matter <= 1.0% (inorganic) and <= 1.0% (organic), Damaged/discolored <= 5.0%, Immature/shriveled <= 3.0%.

C. Mustard & Rapeseed (Brassica juncea):
- Central MSP Rate: ₹5,950 per quintal.
- FAQ Quality Specifications: Minimum oil content 38.0%, Moisture <= 8.0%, Foreign matter <= 2.0%, Damaged/weeviled seeds <= 2.0%.

D. Chickpea / Bengal Gram (Cicer arietinum — Chana):
- Central MSP Rate: ₹5,650 per quintal.
- FAQ Quality Specifications: Moisture <= 10.0%, Foreign matter <= 1.0%, Damaged/shriveled grains <= 3.0%, Weeviled seeds <= 3.0%.

E. Pigeon Pea / Red Gram (Cajanus cajan — Tur / Arhar):
- Central MSP Rate: ₹7,550 per quintal.
- FAQ Quality Specifications: Moisture <= 10.0%, Foreign matter <= 1.0%, Split/broken pulses <= 2.0%.

F. Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum — Kapas):
- Central MSP Rate: Medium Staple: ₹7,121 per quintal; Long Staple: ₹7,521 per quintal.
- FAQ Quality Specifications: Moisture between 8.0% and 12.0%, Trash content <= 7.0%.

G. Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum):
- Central FRP Rate: ₹340 per quintal linked to a basic recovery rate of 10.25%, with a premium of ₹3.32 per quintal for each 0.1% increase in sugar recovery.
